How to read this

A watchlist exists because a credit team can review a fraction of the book, not all of it. The number that matters is how many of next year's defaults sit inside the fraction they can actually review.

Grades come from an internal master scale carrying agency-style labels. They are not a claim that this model's BBB is Standard & Poor's BBB — the PD bands come from the scale design.

PDs are isotonic-calibrated inside each walk-forward fold, with a conservatism overlay on the lowest grades estimated on a held-out prior year.

Panel: American Bankruptcy dataset (Pellegrino et al.), 1999–2018, Kaggle distribution. Obligor identifiers are anonymised in the source data.
